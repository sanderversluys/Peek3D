# Privacy Policy

**Peek3D** does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

The app runs entirely on your Mac as a Quick Look extension. It reads 3D model files (STL, OBJ, 3MF) locally to generate previews and thumbnails. No data leaves your device. No analytics, no tracking, no network requests.
